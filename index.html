Research prototype | Python, LightGBM, FastAPI, Streamlit

BACKLOT: Distributed Product Returns and Recovery Platform

In progress | Python, FastAPI, Redpanda (Kafka-compatible), PostgreSQL, Docker, scikit-learn | All data synthetic

  • Processed 1,740 replayed events with zero duplicates (ledger held at 1,720 processed rows after replaying twice), by building an event-driven pipeline with a per-consumer-group idempotency ledger in PostgreSQL.
  • Kept the pipeline resilient to failures, by adding bounded retries with exponential backoff, a dead-letter topic for poison messages, and offset commits only after processing.
  • Reconstructed return journeys from independent services and flagged missing events, product mismatches and condition conflicts, by building a reconciliation engine that cites the evidence behind every finding.
  • Produced evidence-grounded recommendations for human reviewers, by adding a risk model, a TF-IDF retrieval step over versioned policies, and an optional LLM report with a deterministic fallback.
